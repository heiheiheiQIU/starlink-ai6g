## Schema boundary

`LegacyPrediction` is A1's internal result: raw normalized predictions and provenance for frozen NPZ windows. The evidence JSON is an example, not a `HealthEstimate`. B owns the formal `HealthEstimate` after H00. A1 exposes no online native RUL: `rul_est` is null with `NATIVE_RUL_RESTORE_REQUIRES_FUTURE_RUN_MAX`. Source step indices use cycles and target indices use orbits. Neither is seconds or a SOC, capacity, or energy value. B may choose explicitly synthetic health for simulation.

## Limitations

The original CSV does not reproduce all delivered source NPZ labels; the fixed CSV does. A persisted scaler for new raw telemetry is not established. Native RUL restoration would require a future run maximum. Target-label provenance, online calibration, and satellite operational validity are unverified. These files do not report new communication results.
